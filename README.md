# Arrangix

**Turn a messy pile of photos and screenshots into a clean, ready-to-send Word or PDF file, without ever stretching or cropping a single picture.**

Arrangix is one file, `Arrangix.html`. Open it in your browser, drop your photos in, pick a layout, and download. Your photos never leave your device.

```
  📷 📷 📷 📷 📷          ┌─────────┐         ┌──────────────┐
  🖼️ 📷 🖼️ 📷 📷   ──▶   │ Arrangix │   ──▶   │ Report.pdf   │
  (screenshots, phone     └─────────┘         │ Report.docx  │
   photos, scans…)                            └──────────────┘
```

---

## When do people use it?

| You have… | You want… | Try this |
|---|---|---|
| 40 phone screenshots of receipts or chats | One PDF to email or submit | **Smart**, 2 columns, PDF |
| Photos from an event or a trip | A tidy contact sheet in time order | **Grid** 4×3, sort by **Date taken** |
| Site-inspection or damage photos | A report with the photos in the order you took them | **Grid** 2×2, Word |
| Scans of pages or documents | One file, one scan per page | **Grid** 1×1, PDF |
| iPhone (HEIC) photos | A Word or PDF file anyone can open | Add them as they are |
| A batch with repeated shots | Fewer pages, no copies | Remove the **Similar** photos |
| A set to print at home | Photos that stay clear of the printer's edge | Tick **Printer-safe margin** |

---

## The golden rule

> **A photo is never stretched, squashed, or cropped.** Not to fill a column. Not to fill a page. Not by any feature.

Photos may get bigger or smaller to fit, but their width and height always change together. Rotating a photo only turns it. If a photo does not fill its box, you get white space, not distortion.

---

## 60-second quick start

1. **Add photos.** Click the drop area or drag your photos in. JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and iPhone HEIC all work.
2. **Pick a layout.** Choose **Grid** or **Smart** (see below).
3. **Check the preview.** The summary line tells you how many pages you will get.
4. **Choose Word or PDF** and press **Generate document**. The file downloads straight away.

Optional: type a **File name** first, for example `Trip`, and you get `Arrangix-Trip.pdf`. Leave it empty and you get a dated name.

## Two layouts, one question

> **Do you want a neat, predictable page, or the most photos per page?**

```
        GRID                              SMART
 ┌───────────────┐               ┌───────────────┐
 │ ┌───┐  ┌───┐  │               │ ┌───┐ ┌─────┐ │
 │ │ 1 │  │ 2 │  │               │ │ 1 │ │  2  │ │
 │ └───┘  └───┘  │               │ │   │ └─────┘ │
 │ ┌───┐  ┌───┐  │               │ └───┘ ┌─────┐ │
 │ │ 3 │  │ 4 │  │               │ ┌───┐ │  4  │ │
 │ └───┘  └───┘  │               │ │ 3 │ └─────┘ │
 └───────────────┘               │ └───┘         │
 equal boxes, your order         └───────────────┘
 exact count per page            photos keep their own shape,
                                 packed to fill the page
```

| | **Grid** | **Smart** |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Reports, contact sheets, anything in order | Saving paper, mixed portrait and landscape photos |
| You choose | Rows × Columns | Columns |
| Your order | **Kept** | Smart arranges photos itself |
| Page count | Exactly the number you expect | Usually fewer pages |
| Odd-shaped photos | Shown whole, with white bars if the shape differs | Sized to the column, nothing wasted |
| Auto-rotate | Optional, makes photos bigger in their box | Not used |

**A rule of thumb:** if the order matters, use Grid. If saving pages matters, use Smart.

## What you can do with your photos

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **⠿ Move** | Grab a photo by its dots and drag it. It lifts like an app on a phone's home screen, its old spot becomes a dashed slot, and the others slide aside. **The slot is exactly where it lands.** Esc cancels. |
| **⧉ Duplicate** | Makes a copy right next to the original. |
| **↻ Rotate** | Turns the photo 90° each click. |
| **✕ Remove** | Takes the photo out. |
| **Undo** | Steps back through your last 20 changes (also Ctrl/Cmd+Z). |
| **Sort photos** | **Order added**, **Date taken — oldest first**, or **Date taken — newest first** (Grid only). |
| **Select** | Click photos (Shift-click for a range) to rotate or remove several at once. |
| **Similar finder** | Spots the same picture added twice. The first is tagged **Original A**, the others **Similar A1, A2…** One button removes the similar ones and never the originals. |
| **Soft-print warning** | Warns when a photo is too small to print sharply at its size. |

### Moving photos, step by step

```
 grab the ⠿ dots  ──▶  the photo lifts  ──▶  a dashed slot shows where it will land
                                                          │
         Esc or let go far away = cancel  ◀──  let go = drop into the slot
```

On a phone, only the dots start a move, so swiping anywhere else on a photo still scrolls the page.

## Settings at a glance

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| Rows, Columns | 1 to 10 |
| Gap between photos | 0 to 30 mm |
| Page size | A4, A5, A3, Letter, Square (20×20 cm), or Custom (50 to 558 mm) |
| Orientation | Portrait or Landscape |
| Quality | Standard 150 DPI, High 220, Maximum 300, or Original detail |
| Margin | 6.35 mm, or tick **Printer-safe** for 10 mm |
| Auto-rotate | On or off (Grid only, off by default) |
| Format | Word (.docx) or PDF |

Your settings are remembered next time you open the file. **Reset settings** brings the defaults back.

---

## Good to know

- **Private.** Photos are processed in your browser and are never uploaded.
- **Works offline.** The one exception: iPhone (HEIC) photos in browsers that cannot read them natively need internet to convert. Safari and other Apple browsers do not. If it can't connect, you get a clear notice with **Try again** and the other photos keep working.
- **Printing.** Many home printers cannot print the outer 5 to 6 mm of a page. Tick **Printer-safe margin** to avoid clipped edges.
- **Big files.** **Original detail** keeps every pixel and can make very large files.

---

## What Arrangix deliberately does not do

No cropping. No stretching. No filters or frames. No captions, page numbers, or watermarks. It arranges photos and nothing else, so it stays simple and predictable.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A thumbnail says "could not read" | Read the reason shown next to it. Try re-saving that file as JPEG or PNG. |
| An iPhone photo fails | Check your internet connection and press **Try again**, or convert it to JPEG first. |
| The file is huge | Lower **Quality**, or avoid **Original detail** for big batches. |
| Photos look small in Grid | Use fewer rows or columns, or try **Auto-rotate** for landscape photos in portrait boxes. |
| Sort is greyed out | Smart arranges photos itself. Switch to Grid to keep or sort the order. |

## How well is it tested?

Layouts, exports, rotation, sorting, undo, similar-photo detection and moving photos were tested with automated browser tests. Word output was checked in LibreOffice, and a phone browser was emulated, including touch dragging.

**Not yet tested:** Microsoft Word itself, Google Docs, a real phone, and a real screen reader.
